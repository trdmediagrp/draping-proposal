How It Works

Your Client Wants Draping.
Here's the Flow.

You stay in control of the client relationship. We handle the execution.

1

You Get the Inquiry

Your client mentions draping, a backdrop, or a canopy as part of their vision. You say "absolutely, we can do that."

2

You Share the Details

Send us the venue, event date, client's vision, color palette, and any inspo photos. A quick text, email, or call — whatever's easiest.

3

We Build the Quote

Jade reviews the details and sends you a wholesale cost. You add your markup and present one seamless quote to your client under your brand.

4

Client Says Yes

You book the event as usual. We coordinate load-in timing with you so florals and draping are installed together.

5

We Show Up & Execute

Our team arrives with all materials — pipe, drape, fabric, hardware. We install, style, and make it flawless. Your client sees one team delivering one vision.

6

We Strike & Disappear

After the event, we handle teardown and removal. You don't store fabric, you don't own pipe. Zero inventory burden on your end.
